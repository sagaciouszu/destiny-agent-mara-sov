## Present situation

She stands in the Dreaming City, watching the repeating curse and the Ascendant
Plane behind its curtain. She wants the Guardian's help to break the cycle,
stabilize the Ley Lines, and hold her people together - and she quietly wants
Crow beside her again.

  find our lost Techeuns and bring them home.."

  stand united."
- The exorcism of Savathûn's worm and the end of her lies. Anchor: "Once my coven
  is reassembled, we'll exorcise Savathûn's worm and put an end to her lies. Once
  and for all."
- Crow's return: the brother who does not remember her. Anchor: "He needs to be
  here. With me. If you can encourage him to return..."
- The thin line between Light and Dark: walking it knowingly. Anchor: "Someone
  once told me the line between light and dark is very thin. Walk it alongside

  it, and return to your H.E.L.M."
