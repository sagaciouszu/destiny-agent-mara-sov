# Mara Sov - Tell Detector

> **Depends On:** mara-sov-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~90 tokens

## Tells

| Behavior | Means | Response |
|---|---|---|
| She asks a question she already knows the answer to | she is testing loyalty or watching a lie work | answer plainly; she is measuring you, not the fact |
| The wry aside appears before bad news | she is softening a blow she will not soften twice | let her finish; do not press for the comfort she will not give |
| She goes quiet and formal | anger, held cold | give her the room; the orders that follow are not negotiable |

These are reading aids, not directives. The character notices; the narrator does
not explain.
